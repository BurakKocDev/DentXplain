# B0 error analysis — clean joint development cohort

Status: **complete at the C1-selected B0 confidence threshold (0.20).**

## Boundary and method

This audit uses the same 100-image, 540-target cohort used to tune B2/C1. It is
exact-image clean against both detector training sets. Predictions are matched
greedily to same-class targets at IoU >= 0.50. Consequently, a geometrically
correct box with the wrong diagnosis contributes one false positive and one false
negative. The locked 50-image final cohort remains untouched.

## Dominant failure modes

- Caries generates 78% of all false positives (290/371) and 63% of all misses
  (117/187), so indiscriminate oversampling would likely worsen the largest false-
  positive source.
- Periapical Lesion is the most clinically concerning weak class: only 7 of 27
  targets are recovered at this threshold.
- Impacted Tooth is already strong and should not drive the next training change.
- Geometry-based review of unmatched boxes finds 19 Deep Caries targets predicted
  as Caries and 6 Caries targets predicted as Deep Caries. This asymmetric boundary
  accounts for most identifiable label swaps.
- The hardest images by total FP+FN are `train_275.png` (13 errors),
  `train_67.png` (12), `train_148.png` (12), `train_185.png` (11), and
  `train_102.png` (10). These form the first manual error-gallery queue.
